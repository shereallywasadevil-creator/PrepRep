Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription and no ads.

Where does my information go?

Nowhere. Your profile, plans and weigh-ins are saved on your own computer or phone.

How accurate are the prices?

They're U.S. averages for store-brand groceries, from Bureau of Labor Statistics price data where it exists. Your store may be a little higher or lower, so you can type your own prices in the app.

Can it handle my diet?

Yes. Pick vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, no pork, no red meat or no seafood, and PrepRep only plans meals that fit.

Do my phone and computer share data?

Not yet. Each keeps its own plan on that device.
